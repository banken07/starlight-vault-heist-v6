Starlight Vault

Starlight Vault — steal the Comet Diamond from the midnight observatory. Move with WASD / arrows, sneak with Shift, act with E, tools with 1 / 2 / 3. Three rooms: the Map Room, the Telescope Dome, the Star Vault. Guards MARA and JULES patrol with amber vision cones; brass-eye cameras sweep red cones. Small loot buys tools: lockpick 10 coins, smoke 15, lens cap 8. Pick the glass case, grab the diamond, ride the zipline out before the alarm catches you. Click the floor to walk there. M mute, R restart.
